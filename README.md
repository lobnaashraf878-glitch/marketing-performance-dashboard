Marketing Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing marketing performance, revenue growth, product sales, advertising spend, customer segments, campaign results, and return on investment.

Repository Name

marketing-performance-dashboard

Repository Description


Interactive Power BI dashboard for tracking marketing revenue, product sales, advertising spend, campaign performance, customer segments, and ROI.

Overview

This project provides a consolidated view of marketing and commercial performance. It enables marketing and business teams to evaluate revenue trends, compare campaign sources, monitor advertising efficiency, understand customer composition, and identify the products contributing most to sales.

The dashboard is built in Microsoft Power BI and uses a single source table named Sheet1, containing sales, advertising, campaign, customer, product, and time-related data.

Main Analyses

Revenue Trends

A monthly line chart shows how total revenue changes over time and helps identify growth patterns, seasonal movements, and periods of lower performance.

Customer Analysis

A pie chart presents the revenue contribution of each customer type, helping evaluate the mix of customer segments.

Campaign Performance

Campaign visuals compare advertising sources based on:

•
Campaign revenue

•
Advertising spend

•
Relative marketing efficiency

Product Performance

Product-level visuals compare product types by:

•
Total units sold

•
Total revenue

This helps identify high-volume products and high-value products separately.
